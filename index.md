# Privacy Policy for FloatCounter

**Last Updated:** September 2026

**NyxCodeAssembly** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **FloatCounter** mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and privacy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
**FloatCounter does not collect, track, or share any personally identifiable information or usage analytics.** 

* **Local Calculations:** All float calculations, denomination inputs, and preferences are stored exclusively on your local device and are never transmitted to external servers.
* **Bug Reports & Feedback:** If you explicitly choose to send feedback or crash logs via the in-app option, the app generates a pre-filled draft in your device's default email client. No diagnostic data or emails are sent automatically in the background without your knowledge and manual confirmation.

---

## 2. Permissions
FloatCounter requires minimal device permissions to operate:
* **Internet / Network Access:** Used solely to handle standard system intents (such as launching your email client for feedback).

---

## 3. Third-Party Services
FloatCounter does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.
